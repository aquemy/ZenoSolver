A multi-objective planning benchmark with exact, proven Pareto fronts.

MultiZenoTravel: planes carry passengers from CI to CG through central cities. Every landing has a price, and the cheap cities are the far ones, so each plan trades its cost against its makespan. The best trade-offs form the Pareto front.

ZenoSolver computes that front exactly. For symmetric durations, the front theorem and a solver are proved in Lean 4, and every front of the benchmark suite carries a certificate accepted by a checker proved sound in Lean 4.

The theory in Lean 4

11

results of the theory are machine-checked in Lean 4, 22 more have parts in Lean, and 7 of its definitions are stated in Lean.

No sorry, no native_decide, and no axiom beyond propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

  • Cor. 4.8

    The front of a symmetric instance is the front of the admissible partitions of its landings.

  • Thm 7.5

    The link-forest dynamic program computes the least cost at every makespan.

  • zeno-front

    The verified solver returns exactly the Pareto front of the feasible plans.

  • zeno-check

    The checker accepts a certificate only if its points are the Pareto front.
