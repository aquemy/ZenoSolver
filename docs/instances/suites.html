Benchmark and golden suites

The 66 benchmark instances, the 27 golden instances, and the Lean test sets

Benchmark suites

bench/instances/*.txt hold the instances that every solver is timed on. One instance per line, name|arguments, where the arguments are zeno instance options and @DATA@ stands for bench/instances/data/ (data files in the 2-line format). Lines starting with # are comments.

Suite Instances Content (from the file’s header)
quick 5 Short suite for pull-request CI (each instance well under a second for the legacy solvers).
paper-table1 8 Table 1 of Quemy & Schoenauer, EvoCOP 2015: n = t, p = 2, default generator.
regimes 5 Five regimes of the plan: many cities, few passengers; balanced; p close to t; medium; large. Default generator (c_i = i, d_i = n+1-i).
families 7 Generator families on n = 10, t = 8, p = 3. -C/-D: 1 log, 2 sqrt, 3 identity, 4 power of two, 5 the (5/2)i staircase; -r scales before truncation to integers.
adversarial 5 Instances where greedy rules fail or search is hard: the LPT counterexample, clustered and near-equal durations, multiples, and a 3-Partition gadget (12 cities forming four triples of sum 100, t = 8, p = 4, s = 12).

Golden instances

tests/golden/instances.txt lists 27 tiny symmetric instances (name|arguments, @DATA@ for tests/golden/data/). Their fronts, in tests/golden/fronts/<name>.front as cost makespan lines by increasing cost, were computed by the brute-force oracle zenoOracle (tests/oracle/bruteForce.hpp). The oracle enumerates every PPP, every beta set, and every assignment and order of flights to planes, and simulates the waiting exactly. It is exponential (\(t \le 5\); n = 6 t = 6 p = 2 already takes minutes).

For every instance, CTest checks that the front of zeno equals the golden front (tests/golden/checkFront.cmake) and that zeno check accepts its certificate (checkCertificate.cmake). Pairs known to fail go in tests/golden/known-bugs.txt, currently none; a listed pair that passes fails the suite. To regenerate the fronts:

tests/golden/regenerate.sh build/release/zenoOracle

Instances of note, as documented in the file:

  • lpt-counterexample: \(d = (6, 10)\), \(c = (40, 20)\), \(t = 5\), \(p = 2\). The point (260, 60) is Pareto-optimal, but the greedy of Algorithm 1 (EvoCOP 2015) returns makespan 68 for its PPP. Its front is also proved in Lean by the kernel alone (Examples.lptCounterexample_front, §5).
  • p3-first-counterexample: \(d = (1, 3)\), \(c = (9, 5)\), \(t = 4\), \(p = 2\). The point (42, 12) needs the plane making the drop-off to take the long crossing; zenoSolver_noDuplicate ended at
  • nodup-best-plan and trailing-newline: \(d = (1, 9)\), \(c = (4, 1)\), \(t = 5\), \(p = 3\), read from a file without and with a trailing blank line. The 2016 zenoSolver_noDuplicate kept the last plan of a PPP instead of the best one, and the blank line used to change the instance.
  • The first sixteen use the default generator, including the \(n = t\) instances of Table 1 of the EvoCOP 2015 paper (front sizes 5, 10, 17); the next seven use other families and scales.

Lean test sets

  • lean/test/certificates/accepted/ and rejected/: certificates written by zeno, and corrupted copies with a comment saying what was broken (a wrong cost or duration, a broken route, non-monotone points, a missing point, a forest gap relabelled relaxed, a missing assumption, a truncated file, and version 2 cases such as the Proposition 6.5 instance without its point (14, 74)). bash lean/test/run.sh checks each against its expected verdict.
  • lean/test/instances/: random instances for the end-to-end run (lean/test/e2e.sh), five of them with a forest gap.
  • lean/test/front-regimes.list: the three suite regimes on which zeno-front is compared with zeno front in CI.
