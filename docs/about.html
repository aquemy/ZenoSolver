About

The project, what is proved and how, and how this site is built

The project

ZenoSolver computes the exact Pareto fronts, cost against makespan, of MultiZenoTravel multi-objective planning instances, and proves them. It consists of the solver zeno, the theory note doc/theory/exact-front.md, the Lean 4 formalization in lean/, the certificate format and its two checkers, the benchmarks and the experiments. All are the work of Alexandre Quemy, in the repository aquemy/ZenoSolver.

Honesty: what is proved, and how

The blueprint has 61 nodes. 11 are machine-checked and 7 are Lean definitions. 22 have parts in Lean, and 21 are not in Lean. These badges follow the claim ledger claims.toml: a statement is machine-checked only if Lean proves every assertion of it. The library has 3,675 declarations (2,112 theorems) in 42 modules (Lean v4.36.0, Mathlib v4.36.0). Apart from propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, no declaration uses another axiom: no sorry and no native_decide. These counts are from the last local Lean audit; 12 declarations added since then are badged from the #print axioms footers of their commits, which CI re-checks on every push.

The blueprint’s badges are computed when the site is built, from the claim ledger claims.toml and from collectAxioms on the Lean declarations. A statement is badged machine-checked only if the ledger records it so, which requires Lean to prove every assertion of it, and only if those declarations are axiom-clean theorems. When Lean proves part of a statement, the node is badged parts in Lean, lists those declarations, and says in one line what they miss. The generator stops if a declaration is missing, a label is not found in the note, or a machine-checked claim has an unclean declaration, and the claim graph’s build checks that the ledger’s cached statuses are the ones its history gives. The same badges appear next to every theorem cited in the paper.

The compiled tools add trust in the Lean compiler and in an unverified parser (trust base).

This website

Built with Quarto from site/ into docs/ (how). Generated, never typed by hand:

  • the blueprint, from site/blueprint.toml, the theory note and the Lean sources (site/gen_blueprint.py);
  • the result tables, from the committed benchmark baselines (site/gen_pages.py);
  • the claim graph and its history, from the knowledge commits and claims.toml (site/gen_graph.py);
  • the plans of the problem page, the instance fronts and the figures of the algorithm page, computed with zeno (site/gen_problem.py, site/gen_fronts.py, site/gen_algorithm.py).
